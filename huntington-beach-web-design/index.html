06 / A FEW LOCAL QUESTIONS

Good to know before we start.

Are you actually based in Huntington Beach?

Yes. Functional Websites is a licensed Huntington Beach business. I’m Cooper, and I was born and raised here. I’m still based in Huntington Beach.

How much does a small-business website cost?

A basic site starts at a $1,500 planning estimate with content and design direction supplied. The final price depends on your pages, design, and features. We agree on the scope and price before work begins.

Can you rebuild my current WordPress website?

Yes, when static is a good fit. I’ll review the pages and features you rely on first. Member accounts, live inventory, and frequent publishing can need a different approach or additional integrations.

Will my website show up on Google?

I build with search-friendly basics: readable pages, descriptive titles, clear headings, and useful content about your business. Google controls indexing and rankings; a website build does not guarantee a position in search results.

Do I own the finished website?

You receive the agreed HTML, CSS, and asset files. You can move them to a host that supports static websites. Domain, hosting, and any third-party services are separate.
