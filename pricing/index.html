Before you request a quote

Is $1,500 a fixed price?

No. It is a starting planning estimate for a new build. We agree on the scope and final price before work begins.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. Share your current URL and what you want to improve. We can discuss a focused cleanup or a rebuild.

Can I host the finished website elsewhere?

Yes. Static website files are portable. Hosting and any external services are discussed as part of your project.

What should I include in my request?

Your business, current website if you have one, the pages or features you need, and your preferred timing.
